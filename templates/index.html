System Overview

SafeVision AI is a real-time PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) compliance monitoring system, developed for the NUTECH–NESCOM project. Powered by a custom-trained YOLOv8 deep learning model, it automatically detects safety helmets, vests, gloves, boots, and eyewear across uploaded images/videos and live camera feeds — flagging violations instantly and generating downloadable PDF audit reports to help site supervisors enforce workplace safety standards. Registered users who upload a profile photo are also identified by name on the live camera feed.
